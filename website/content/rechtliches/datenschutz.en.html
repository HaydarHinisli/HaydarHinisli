Protecting your data matters to me. This website is deliberately built to process as little data as possible: there are no cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising and no embedded content from other providers. Below I explain which data is still processed, why, and on what legal basis.

No data protection officer has been appointed, as there is no legal obligation to do so. For any privacy questions, please contact the email address above.

2. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. When you visit the website, your browser automatically transmits information to the server, which is stored in server log files:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of access
  • page or file requested
  • amount of data transferred and status code (e.g. "successful", "not found")
  • referring website
  • browser and operating system used

This data is processed solely to deliver the website, to ensure its stable and secure operation and to detect attacks or malfunctions. It is not combined with other data or analysed for marketing purposes. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is the technically sound and secure provision of the website.

The host deletes the log files, or anonymises the IP addresses in them, after no more than 7 days, unless they are needed longer to investigate a specific security incident. The host's statistics or analytics features are disabled.

A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with IONOS. IONOS processes the data only on my instructions; the website is hosted in a data centre in Germany.

3. Encryption

For security reasons, this website uses TLS encryption (recognisable by "https://" and the padlock icon in your browser), so transmitted data cannot be read by third parties.

4. No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use any analytics, tracking or advertising services. No user profiles are created.

All fonts, images and scripts are loaded directly from this server. Visiting the website does not establish a connection to other providers (e.g. Google Fonts, content delivery networks, video platforms or social networks).

5. Storage in your browser (localStorage)

For two convenience features, the website stores small entries in your browser's local storage (localStorage). These entries remain solely on your device, are not transmitted to me or third parties, contain no identifier and cannot be linked to you:

  • "4e-intro-seen": the date on which the short intro on the home page was shown, so that it does not play again on every visit. The entry is disregarded after 30 days.
  • "4e-theme": your choice between light and dark view, if you used the switch in the header.

The legal basis is Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act), as storing and reading these entries is strictly necessary to provide the features you use (keeping your chosen view, not repeating the intro on every visit). As the entries contain no personal data, the GDPR does not apply to them. You can delete these entries at any time by clearing the site data in your browser; the website works fully without them.

6. Contact by email

If you send me an email, I process the information it contains (e.g. name, email address, content of your message and, where applicable, details of your workflows and tools) in order to answer your enquiry and, if applicable, to prepare an offer or carry out an assignment.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the enquiry relates to a contract or pre-contractual steps, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries).

The data is deleted once the enquiry has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Correspondence relevant under commercial and tax law (e.g. offers, orders, invoices) is retained for the statutory periods of up to ten years (Section 257 HGB, Section 147 AO).

Please note that emails are not always fully protected against access by third parties in transit. Therefore, please do not send login details or passwords by email; we will exchange them securely if needed.

7. Recipients and disclosure of data

Your data is only received by service providers who support me on my instructions, namely my hosting and email provider IONOS SE and – where invoices are concerned – my tax advisor, who is bound by professional secrecy. Beyond that, I only disclose data if I am legally obliged to do so or if you have expressly consented. No data is transferred to countries outside the European Union.

Providing your data is neither a legal nor a contractual requirement. Without the information in your email, however, I cannot answer your enquiry.

8. Your rights

Within the scope of the law, you have the right at any time to:

  • access the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): Where processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object to it at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

To exercise your rights, an informal message to haydar@4elements-digital.de is sufficient.
